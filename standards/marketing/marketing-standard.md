# PROXTEL Marketing Standard v1.0

Marketing work must be source-grounded, measurable and explicit about objectives, audiences, claims, evidence and consent boundaries.

Campaigns and content must distinguish verified facts from proposals. Unsupported or misleading claims are forbidden.

Automatic campaign publishing, automatic ad spend and sensitive-attribute targeting are disabled by default.

CRM governs CRM-domain records, Sales governs commercial semantics, Finance governs financial boundaries, Documentation governs reusable documentation and Development owns repository implementation.
