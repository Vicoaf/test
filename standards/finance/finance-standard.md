# PROXTEL Finance Standard v1.0

Finance work must be source-grounded, auditable and explicit about assumptions, scenarios and accounting boundaries.

Financial facts require authoritative evidence. Budget and forecast assumptions must be distinguishable from actuals. Billing, revenue and reconciliation must preserve traceability.

No autonomous transfer of funds, banking action or production mutation is allowed by default.

CRM governs CRM-domain records, Sales governs sales-domain semantics, Documentation governs reusable documentation and Development owns repository implementation.
