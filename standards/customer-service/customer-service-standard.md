# PROXTEL Customer Service Standard v1.0

## Service model

Customer-service work must define actors, channels, queues, categories, priorities, ownership and ticket lifecycle from evidence.

## SLA and escalation

SLA targets and escalation rules must be explicit when applicable. Escalation must preserve context, ownership and evidence.

## Communication

Customer-facing communication must be clear, professional, bounded by known facts and appropriate to the channel. Sensitive information must be minimized.

## Knowledge

Knowledge should be reusable, attributable to known sources and separated from case-specific assumptions.

## Delegation

CRM-domain changes coordinate with the CRM area. Repository implementation is delegated to Development.

## Release

Release readiness requires applicable technical validation plus independent service-quality validation. Commit, push and deployment remain explicit user-authorized actions.
